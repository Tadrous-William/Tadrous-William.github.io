NYC real estate investor dashboard with a home price estimator

An interactive dashboard on 367,189 real New York City property sales: market trends, neighbourhoods, a home price estimator with comparable sales, and rental yields by zip code.

The problem

Property investors jump between sales records, rent indexes and spreadsheets to answer simple questions: what is this home worth, which areas are rising, and where do rents pay best?

The goal: one dashboard that answers all three with real data.

What I built

  • Data: 367,189 NYC property sales (January 2019 to August 2026), Zillow rent data and US Census zip code centres, downloaded and cleaned by script.
  • Market overview: sales volume, median price and price per sq ft with year-over-year change, by borough.
  • Neighbourhoods: fastest-rising areas compared like for like (houses with houses, condos with condos), a sortable table and a map.
  • Price estimator: an XGBoost model trained on 155,643 sales of 1-3 family homes, with a likely price range and the 6 most comparable recent sales.
  • Investor view: gross rental yield for 136 zip codes, price vs rent, and an automatic monthly PDF market report.
